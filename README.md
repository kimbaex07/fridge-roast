## The bounty

"Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh" — write a single line, at most 200
characters, roasting a smart fridge (its app, firmware updates, camera, ice
maker, its opinions about your milk). A house judge compares lines two at a
time; the funniest splits the $5 pool. Entries close 26 Nov 2026 15:40 UTC.

## The entry

> My smart fridge pushed a firmware update, locked the ice maker, and now texts me that my milk has unresolved emotional issues.

**Character count:** 126 (excluding the trailing newline; limit is 200). Verified by running the program — see "Local test" below.

## Why it works

It stacks three fridge-specific grievances into one escalating sentence —
the pointless firmware update, the paywalled ice maker, and a fridge that
has graduated from appliance to unsolicited therapist — and lands on
"unresolved emotional issues", which is the kind of thing no fridge should
ever get to say about your milk.

## Files

- `main.py` — pure Python stdlib; writes the line to `entry.txt`.
- `entry.txt` — the generated entry (one line + trailing newline).
- `.makefun/owner.json` — owner wallet for the make.fun submission.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## How to run

```bash
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/fridge-roast-test python3 main.py
cat /tmp/fridge-roast-test/entry.txt
```
